The difference

Messy speech in, text you can send as-is, out.

Not word-for-word. It groups the scattered bits into one point, puts them in order, and writes it in the register you picked.

What you said into the phone
“So… um, the schedule for this round, like design's gonna take like two more days, and then dev's actually okay, uh testing might have to get squeezed, anyway basically push the whole thing back like three days.”
What Transless put on screen
Here's where the timeline stands:
  1. Design needs roughly two extra days.
  2. Development is on track.
  3. QA will have to be compressed.
Net effect: the schedule shifts out by about three days.
How it works

Three steps. There is no fourth.

Hold and talk

Pause, change your mind, think mid-sentence. Tap to stop when you're done.

It rewrites for you

Fillers gone, points merged, rewritten in Casual, Work or Email register.

Straight onto the screen

It's a keyboard. The result lands in the box you were already typing in — WhatsApp, email, any app.

Privacy

What you saywe don't keep.

Every line below maps to behaviour you can find in the code. Change the backend and this page has to change too.

Audio is dropped once recognised

Audio is uploaded only to work out what you said. The moment that's done it isdiscarded immediately — never written to disk, no copies kept.

Results live in memory for 10 minutes

The finished text sits in memory for your phone to fetch,10 minutes at most, then it's cleared. A restart wipes it. It never touches disk.

No history, no profiling

We keep no conversation archive and build no user profile. We alsodon't ask foryour contacts, photos or location.

Nobody is reading what you say

The abuse checks compliance requires areautomated rule matching — no human reads it. We don't sell or rent your content, and we don't use it for ads.

But there's one thing we have to be straight about: To turn messy speech into idiomatic English, the recognised texthas to go to an AI model provider. There's no way around that step, and we're not going to pretend otherwise. So: Don't dictate passwords, card numbers, ID numbers, or anything under an NDA.

FAQ

The things you're probably wondering

Does it need a connection? Can I use it offline?

Yes, it needs one. Recognition and rewriting run on our servers. Handwriting input is the exception — that model runs on your phone.

Is what I say stored anywhere?

No. Audio is discarded the moment it's recognised; the result sits in memory for 10 minutes at most. We hold no history of yours.

Do I need an account?

Not now — install and go. Sign-in is coming so you can restore things on a new phone. It won't be mandatory; it'll still work without one.

Which languages are supported?

Nine today: English, Japanese, French, German, Spanish, Korean, Cantonese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese. Speak, pick a target language, done. Read-aloud picks a native voice to match the text — Japanese output gets a Japanese voice, not an English accent.

Which apps does it work in?

It's a real keyboard, not a separate translation app. Use it anywhere you can type — WhatsApp, email, notes, your browser.

What if it gets the translation wrong?

AI translation makes mistakes. That's why there's a built-in typing keyboard (Pinyin / Wubi / handwriting) so you can fix anything on the spot — or switch to Verbatim, which changes nothing except punctuation.

How do I remove it completely?

Turn Transless off in your system keyboard settings first, then uninstall as usual. Uninstalling stops all processing — there's nothing on our side for you to delete.
