1. What we process

Transless is a keyboard/input method that turns your spoken Chinese into English (or another target language). To do that, we process:

TypeWhat it isWhy we need it
VoiceThe audio recorded while you hold the speak buttonTo recognize what you said
TextThe recognized text and the generated translationTo rewrite and translate it
Device identifierA random ID generated the first time the app launches (a device token)Abuse prevention, per-device free-quota accounting, and usage counting

About that device ID: it is generated randomly by the app and does not contain your phone number, IMEI, advertising identifier, or any identity information from your phone — we cannot use it to work out who you are. Its only purpose is letting our server tell that requests are coming from the same device. It stops meaning anything once you uninstall the app.

We do not collect: your name, government ID, location, contacts, photo library, advertising identifiers, or system-level device identifiers (IMEI / Android ID / IDFA).
(The phone number or email used to sign in is covered separately under "Account information" below.)

Account information

Account sign-in is live. The core features work without signing in; signing in is only required for features that need to recognize you (for example, paid quota). Sign-in uses your phone number or email + a one-time code — there is no password.

When you create an account, we record:

  • The phone number or email you sign in with;
  • Four signals used only to count users by country: your device's region setting, time zone, language, and a two-letter country code derived from your IP address at that moment.

🔒 We do not store your IP address, and we never send it to any third party — the country code (e.g. HK, CN) is looked up on our own server against an offline address range table; your IP never leaves our server, and only those two letters are kept afterward.

We look at all four signals together because any single one can be misleading (a region setting can be changed manually, an IP can be masked by a VPN) — cross-checking them gives us a reasonably accurate country breakdown.

🔒 Phone-number sign-in uses Alibaba Cloud's phone-verification service — the one-time code is generated and checked by Alibaba Cloud; we never see those 6 digits ourselves.

Not signing in does not affect the core features.

2. Because this is a keyboard, one thing needs to be said plainly

A keyboard is technically able to read anything typed into any text field. Transless needs you to enable "Allow Full Access" in system settings in order to work online. So we're drawing the line explicitly:

  • We only start recording and uploading audio after you actively press the speak button.
  • At every other moment, we do not read, record, or upload anything you type.
  • The "translate the Chinese text before the cursor" feature reads a snippet of text before the cursor only when you actively tap it.
  • We do not log keystrokes, and we do not collect your typing patterns for any kind of analysis.

3. Where this goes, and how long we keep it

1. Your voice is uploaded to our server, which forwards it to our speech-recognition vendor. Our server never writes your voice or text to disk — it is discarded as soon as it has been processed.
(Usage logging is a separate matter — it holds only a timestamp, which feature was used, and a hashed identifier, and contains none of your content; see §10.)

2. The recognized text is sent to an AI model vendor (see §4) to be rewritten and translated.

3. The generated translation (and any synthesized speech from the read-aloud feature) sits briefly in our server's memory while your device retrieves it. The moment your device picks it up, we mark it for deletion and it is automatically wiped within 1 minute; if your device never picks it up at all (say, the connection drops), it is wiped after 3 minutes at the latest.

Why we hold it at all: we use a "submit the job, then poll for the result" pattern, because our gateway times out on long-running requests — so the result has to sit somewhere until your device comes back for it. It only ever lives in memory; a server restart erases it, and it is never written to disk.
Why we keep it for 1 minute after pickup instead of deleting it immediately: if the result gets lost on the way back to you, your device will retry — deleting it immediately would make it look like nothing came back after you spoke. That 1 minute exists for retries.

4. We do not build any history log, conversation archive, or user profile.

Different kinds of data go to different places — to be precise:

  • Your voice and text content → mainland China (DeepSeek, Volcano Engine). If you are outside mainland China, this is a cross-border transfer.
  • Your account information (sign-in phone/email, regional signals) → Singapore (Supabase).
  • One-time code delivery → email goes via the United States (Resend), phone codes go via mainland China (Alibaba Cloud).

Each of these vendors' handling of data is governed by its own privacy policy.

Given the above, we recommend you avoid dictating:

  • Passwords, bank card numbers, government ID numbers, or other sensitive personal information
  • Trade secrets or content covered by a confidentiality agreement

We do not sell, rent, or use your content for advertising.

The app does not include any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK. Handwriting recognition runs entirely on your device and does not go online.

5. Your data is not used to train models

We ourselves never use your voice or text to train any model, and never use it for advertising, selling, or renting.

For third-party vendors, we're stating this honestly rather than glossing over it:

  • DeepSeek (primary processor for text rewriting and translation) — its open-platform terms do not explicitly state whether API data is used for training. We cannot make that promise on its behalf, so we're telling you that plainly rather than offering a guarantee we can't back up.
  • Volcano Engine (speech recognition; backup channel for text processing) — its public commitment states that "Volcano Engine will not access or use your data without your authorization," and it provides end-to-end encryption and isolation.
Why we include this section at all: we would rather be precise than make a commitment we cannot guarantee. We review this section periodically; last reviewed 2026-08-25.

6. What we don't have

These are things many similar products do that we deliberately do not do:

  • No third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK anywhere in the app.
  • No tracking scripts on our website (no Google Analytics, no ad pixels).
  • We do not collect your browsing history, visited URLs, window titles, or which other apps you use.
  • Handwriting recognition runs entirely on your device — your handwriting is never uploaded.

7. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted end-to-end over HTTPS.
  • Our server never writes your voice or text to disk — it only ever passes through memory.
  • The keys used to call third-party services live only on our server and are never shipped inside the app.

8. A note on AI-generated content

Translations are generated by AI and may contain errors or omissions. Please review them before sending. The app clearly marks this wherever a translation is shown.

9. Content moderation

As required by applicable law and by our vendors, we run automated checks for policy-violating content on both what you say and what is generated. This is programmatic keyword- and rule-based matching — no human reads your content as part of this process.

10. Your rights

We do not retain your voice or text content, so there is no content archive for us to let you query, export, or delete. Uninstalling the app stops all processing at any time.

You may ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting the email address below. Once we receive that request, we delete the account itself along with the information that directly identifies you (the sign-in phone number or email).

For usage accounting, abuse prevention, and service reliability, we retain a usage log that contains no content — only a timestamp, which feature was used, and a salted, hashed identifier. Once your account is deleted, we can no longer link that log back to you.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, our legal bases for processing are: processing your voice and text is based on necessity for performing our contract with you (you asked us to translate it); processing the device identifier is based on our legitimate interest (preventing abuse, keeping the service running). The transfer to mainland China is based on necessity for performing the contract (GDPR Article 49(1)(b)).

If you are a California resident: we do not sell or "share" (as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA) your personal information, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise any right, or with questions about this policy, contact: support@transless.net.
